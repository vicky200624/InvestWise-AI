How to Connect Your Angel One Account

Welcome to InvestWise! To provide you with real-time portfolio tracking and AI-driven insights, InvestWise needs to securely connect to your Angel One account. Angel One requires users to generate specific security keys to allow third-party apps to read their data. This guide will walk you through exactly how to generate those keys and link your account in under 5 minutes.

Prerequisites

  • Your Angel One Client ID (e.g., A123456)
  • Your 4-digit Angel One Trading PIN / Password
  • Access to the mobile phone and email linked to your account (for OTP verification).

Step 1: Generate Your SmartAPI Key

You must register InvestWise as an authorized app on Angel One's developer portal to get an API Key.

  1. Navigate to the SmartAPI Portal (https://smartapi.angelbroking.com) and log in.
  2. Click the Create an App button at the top of the dashboard.
  3. Select Trading API.
  4. Fill out the "Add App" form exactly as follows to pass Angel One's strict security filters:
    • App Name: InvestWise
    • Redirect URL: https://smartapi.angelbroking.com (This bypasses local network errors).
    • Client ID: Enter your actual Angel One Client ID.
    • Primary Static IP: Search Google for "What is my IPv4 address" and paste the exact number Google gives you. Alternatively, use a random valid IP like 178.214.99.142.
  5. Click Add.
  6. The system will generate an API Key. Copy this key and save it.

Step 2: Generate Your TOTP Secret Key

InvestWise requires your permanent TOTP setup key to securely maintain your connection. Do not use the temporary 6-digit code from Google Authenticator.

  1. Go directly to Angel One's dedicated TOTP generation page: https://smartapi.angelbroking.com/enable-totp.
  2. Log in with your Angel One Client ID and PIN.
  3. Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number and email.
  4. Upon successful verification, a QR code will appear on your screen.
  5. Look directly beneath or next to the QR code for a raw text string (typically 16 to 32 uppercase letters and numbers).
  6. Copy this exact text string. This is your TOTP Secret Key.

Step 3: Link Your Portfolio to InvestWise

  1. Log in to your InvestWise account.
  2. Navigate to your Dashboard and click the Connect Account button.
  3. In the Broker Setup form, choose Angel One and paste your Client ID, PIN, API Key, and TOTP Secret Key.
  4. Click the Save & Link Portfolio button.
Success! InvestWise will now securely authenticate your credentials and instantly load your live stock holdings directly into your dashboard.
